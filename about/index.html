About

Mobile dirty soda co.

The Dirty Yeti serves dirty sodas, energy drinks and lemonades out of a pink camper in Indiana.

Yes, we’re THAT pink camper.

If you’ve spotted a pink camper with a yeti on the side, that was us. We park, open the window and start pouring.

We don’t stay in one place, so the best way to find us is to follow along on Facebook and Instagram.

So what’s a dirty soda?

A dirty soda is a soda that’s been mixed up: flavored syrups, plus cream or creamer, sometimes finished with cold foam or whipped cream.

Ours start with sodas like Dr Pepper, Coke, root beer, cream soda and Sprite. If soda isn’t your thing, we also do energy drinks and lemonades.
