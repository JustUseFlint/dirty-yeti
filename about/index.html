About The Dirty Yeti

Yes, we’re THAT pink camper.

A mobile dirty soda co. based in Indiana.

The Dirty Yeti serves dirty sodas, energy drinks and lemonades out of a pink camper. We park, open the window and start pouring. Then we move on, so the best way to find us is to follow along.

The short version

What’s a dirty soda?

A dirty soda is a soda mixed with flavored syrups and a splash of cream or creamer, usually over ice. Some get a topping like cold foam or whipped cream.

The idea took off in Utah’s soda shops and has spread from there. Ours start with sodas like Dr Pepper, Coke, root beer, cream soda and Sprite.

The mascot

Meet the yeti.

He’s on the side of the camper and on every cup: a big white yeti with a wide grin, holding a pink soda with whipped cream and cherries on top. He’s the reason it’s The Dirty Yeti.

And yes, there’s a drink named after him. The Yeti is Coke, vanilla, cherry and cold foam.
