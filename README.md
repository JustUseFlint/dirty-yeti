# The Dirty Yeti · Dirty Soda Co. — website

Static site for **The Dirty Yeti**, a mobile dirty soda camper in Indiana ("Yes, we're THAT pink camper").
Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/people/The-Dirty-Yeti/61586227516939/ · Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/thedirtyyeti_/ · Email: thedirtyyeti.dirtysodaco@gmail.com

Live: https://justuseflint.github.io/dirty-yeti/

## Pages
Home, Menu (a page for every drink; category anchors like `menu/#energy` and old `menu/#the-yeti` links redirect to the drink page), Find us, Events, About, 404.

## Stack
Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, generated by `node _build/build.mjs` from `_build/data.mjs` (menu and facts), `_build/pages.mjs`, `_build/shell.mjs` and `_build/cup.mjs` (the illustrated cups).
Motion: GSAP + ScrollTrigger for one-time reveals, vendored in `assets/vendor/`. Scrolling is native. Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque and Figtree (Google Fonts). Respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
Bump `V` in `_build/data.mjs` to cache-bust CSS/JS.

## Content sources
Everything on the site comes from the Facebook page and the menu boards. Nothing is invented: no hours, phone, address, reviews or catering packages.
- Menu: two menu-board photos. Purple Rain and Orange Dreamsicle descriptions are cut off in the photo, so they show "Ask at the window". Blue Voltage and Strawberry Shortcake are cut off after the listed ingredients and show "+ more".
- Jack Frost: new seasonal drink, no recipe or price published.
- Recent stop: Walmart, 1835 US 231, Crawfordsville, Nov 4–7 and Nov 10–14, 11 AM–5 PM (from the posted schedules). Not a permanent location.

## Images
Real, from the Facebook page (EXIF/GPS stripped, 800 + up-to-1600 JPG/WebP, never upscaled, by `_build/images.py`):
`cover`, `drink-1..4`, `truck-1`, `schedule-1/2`. Logo cut out from the profile image (`logo-240/640`, favicon, apple-touch-icon, `og.jpg`).
The drink cups are SVG illustrations drawn in `_build/cup.mjs`; colors follow each drink's ingredients.
